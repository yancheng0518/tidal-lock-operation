# Compatibility renderer art

The harbour, sky, guard and equipment sheets are pre-rendered views of the Tidal Lock level and its original in-game models. They have been rendered from multiple angles and reduced to texture atlases for the browser's software rendering path.

The guard, weapon, sleeves, gloves and sight use the works by **DanlyVostok**, **BAMEN**, **Firewarden**, **bumstrum (DJMaesen)**, **ccransh**, **valterjherson1**, **Quaternius**, and the **100STYLE** authors credited in the [character and equipment credits](../actor-assets/CREDITS.md). The underlying model and motion licensing and original links are listed there, including [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The photographed surface and sky sources used for the environment are credited in the [environment credits](../world-assets/CREDITS.md).
